Hasse's bound

The number of points can't stray far from p + 1. Hasse's theorem says

| #E(𝔽p) − (p + 1) | ≤ 2√p,

or, in terms of the trace of Frobenius ap = p + 1 − #E(𝔽p), simply |ap| ≤ 2√p. Move the sliders: the count always lands in the interval above.

Every prime at once

Now take a and b as ordinary integers and count points mod every prime up to 4000. Dividing each ap by 2√p squeezes them all into [−1, 1].
