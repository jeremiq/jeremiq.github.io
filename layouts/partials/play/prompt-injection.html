Ledger is a scripted simulation: a few pattern-matching rules stand in for a gullible assistant, so each level can show one real defense and one real way around it. No AI model is involved, and nothing you type leaves your browser. The secrets are in the page source. That is a lesson of its own, and it's fine to look, but it's more fun not to.
